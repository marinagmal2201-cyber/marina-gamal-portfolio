About Me

Bridging the gap between design and real-world application

I am an Electrical Power Engineer with a strong focus on Fire Alarm and ELV systems, combining technical knowledge with practical field experience.

Throughout my work and studies, I have developed a deep understanding of fire alarm system design, including notification devices, NAC circuits, control modules and system integration based on international standards such as NFPA 72.

I am particularly interested in not just understanding how systems work, but why certain engineering decisions are made, and how to optimize them based on cost, availability and project requirements.

  • Designing reliable and code-compliant systems
  • Understanding real site challenges and practical constraints
  • Simplifying complex technical concepts for better communication
